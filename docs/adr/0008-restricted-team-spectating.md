# Restrict spectating to the participant's own team

Death puts a participant into Conclave-controlled spectating with no noclip or independent scouting and no visibility beyond their own raid team's permitted information. This requires explicit camera and data-visibility restrictions rather than simply granting unrestricted spectator controls, so death cannot become a way to bypass an encounter's information constraints. Camera behavior and access to another participant's private displays are refined separately.

The user replaced this policy with [revival at a grave followed by configurable spectating](0009-grave-revival-before-spectating.md). The old blanket spectator restriction no longer governs the post-revival-window state.
